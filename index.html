Welcome to Sniffy's World!

Meet Sniffy, a 10-year-old rescue dog with the personality of an old grandpa. He's shy, independent, and enjoys cuddling on his own terms.

Sniffy was adopted eight years ago from a shelter in Santa Clarita, California. This website shares his story, our adventures, and what I've learned about caring for him.
